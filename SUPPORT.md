# Getting help and reporting problems

1. **Read the Check Your Install section** in `README.txt` / `README.md`. Start-up problems may be a missing
   or wrong `dinput.dll` (it must be the 32-bit loader renamed to `dinput.dll`), or a missing `global.ini`, if using MO2.
2. **Open an issue** (Issues tab, "New issue") and pick *Bug report* (crash or won't start) or
   *Limit or glitch report* (powers, talents, bolt-ons, team bonuses, skins). Fill in the form.
3. **Attach the logs and settings**: `xml2_roster.log`, `xml2_chain.log` and `rosterhack.ini` (or a screenshot of its settings). `xml2_roster.log` is next to `rosterhack.ini` (usually the plugins folder). `xml2_chain.log` is in the game folder, next to `XMen2.exe`.
   Close the game first so the files are complete, and save and close any open copy of `rosterhack.ini` (Notepad can show settings the game did not actually load).

## What the logs contain
Only game data: hero names the game loads, counts, memory sizes and (for crashes) addresses inside the game's own files.
They stay on your computer, and the hack never sends anything anywhere. They do not record your folder paths.
Never post anything personal.

## What helps most
- The exact team and place when it happened, and whether it repeats every time.
- Whether it also happens with the hack removed.
- What you changed just before it started.
